## What actually works

Three patterns dominate legitimate “free Steam / gaming rewards.” Magic generators and “instant free codes” are **not** successful long-term models.

### Concept 1 — In-house GPT / rewards shell (product template — ADOPTED AS UX)

**Examples:** Gamesbolt, Earnit.gg, Freeward, Idle-Empire, Rewardly, GameTame

**Shared model:**
1. User creates account on *your* site  
2. Earns **your** points via surveys, videos, app/game offers (offerwalls)  
3. Redeems for Steam gift cards, game keys, skins, Discord Nitro, crypto  
4. Trust via Trustpilot, live “recently rewarded” feeds, clear How-it-works, ~$5 minimum cashout  

**Gamesbolt patterns we will copy:**
- Quest framing (“complete quests → rewards”)
- Redeem catalog (Steam-first for Vaultquest MVP)
- Instant or clearly timed fulfillment messaging
- Live social-proof ticker of recent rewards (real data only)
- FAQ with honest time expectations

### Concept 2 — Big branded GPT + creator CPA (growth template — ADOPTED FOR TRAFFIC)

**Example:** Freecash (+ Impact-style partner deals)

**Model:** Creators send traffic; earn roughly **$3–$10 CPA per signup** and/or **5–30% ongoing** of referral earnings.

**Our adaptation:** Creators and ads send users to **Vaultquest**, not only raw Freecash. Freecash (and peers) sit in the **rotator** behind our Earn UI and/or as a featured partner quest.

### Concept 3 — Hybrid (LOCKED)

Own site + Vault points + multi-offerwall + rotating CPA/referral links + honest giveaways + auto failover on caps.

## Playbook table (copy / adapt)

| Pattern | Where seen | Why it converts | Our adaptation | Blind-copy risk |
|--------|------------|-----------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Own currency + redeem catalog | Gamesbolt, Earnit, Rewardly | Feels like a product | Vault points + Steam-first catalog | Over-promising AAA speed |
| Multi-offerwall earn page | Idle-Empire, Freeward | Fill rate + fallback | Waterfall + auto rotation | Double-credit across walls |
| Live recent redemptions | Gamesbolt | Social proof | Real feed only | Fake feed = trust death |
| Honest time-to-reward FAQ | Gamesbolt | Cuts rage | Required on How-it-works | — |
| Steam SEO landing pages | Freeward + many GPTs | Capture intent | Guides that beat scam generators | Keyword stuffing |
| Creator → signup CPA | Freecash Impact | Scales traffic | CTA to Vaultquest, then partners | Sending traffic only to Freecash |
| Gaming-niche branding | Rewardly, GameTame | Trust | Vaultquest gaming voice | Casino/scam look |
| Streaks / daily / raffles | Freeward, Freecash | Retention | Light daily + scheduled giveaways | Expensive giveaway addiction |

## Never copy

Generators, “no survey” lies, contact-gated codes, password phishing, single dead affiliate links, gestyy-style spam shortlinks as primary CTA.
