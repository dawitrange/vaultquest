## On-camera / VO script

**[0:00 — HOOK]**  
**On-screen:** `GENERATORS ARE FAKE`  
If you’ve been searching “free Steam gift card” or “free Steam wallet,” here’s the truth in five seconds: **code generators do not work.** They’re scams.  

**[0:08]**  
**On-screen:** `HONEST PATH → VAULTQUEST`  
What *does* work is slower and boring — and real. You earn by completing quests and offers, bank **Vault points**, then unlock Steam credit or keys from the vault. That’s Vaultquest. Link’s in the description.

**[0:22 — WHAT WE ARE / AREN’T]**  
**On-screen:** `NO GENERATORS · NO PASSWORDS`  
We’re not a magic code machine. We don’t ask for your Steam password. Ever. Rewards are partner-funded — meaning when you finish real tasks, partners pay, and that funds what you redeem.

**[0:35 — DISCLOSURE]**  
**On-screen:** `AFFILIATE / PARTNER LINKS`  
Quick disclosure: some links may be affiliate or partner links. We may earn when you sign up or complete offers. That’s how this stays honest instead of fake.

**[0:45 — 3 STEPS]**  
**On-screen:** `1 · QUESTS`  
Step one — create your Vaultquest account and pick a quest. That can be a game offer, a survey, videos — whatever’s live for your region. I’m not going to pretend there’s “no survey.” Sometimes surveys are in the mix. Pick what fits your time.

**[1:00]**  
**On-screen:** `2 · VAULT POINTS`  
Step two — finish the quest the way it’s written. No VPN tricks, no multi-accounts — that just gets you banned and helps nobody. Points hit your balance when the partner confirms.

**[1:12]**  
**On-screen:** `3 · UNLOCK STEAM`  
Step three — redeem. Hit the threshold, unlock Steam credit or keys from the vault — or enter a fair giveaway if that’s live. Clear rules. No “DM me after Code #1.”

**[1:25 — DEMO BEAT]**  
**On-screen:** `CONCEPT DEMO` or live site if ready  
I’ll show the flow on screen: Home → Earn → points → Rewards. If you’re watching before launch, this is the coming-soon / concept path — join from the link so you’re ready when quests go live.

**[1:40 — TIME EXPECTATIONS]**  
**On-screen:** `TIME VARIES · NOTHING GUARANTEED`  
How long to first Steam credit? It depends on offers, your country, and how fast you finish. Some people clear a small card in a few hours with a strong game offer. A lot of people take longer. Anyone promising “instant free fifty dollars” is lying.

**[1:55 — CTA]**  
**On-screen:** `LINK IN DESCRIPTION` + `vaultquest.com`  
Want the honest path? Tap the link in the description — Vaultquest. Earn gaming rewards honestly. Complete quests, build Vault points, unlock from the vault.

**[2:05 — OUTRO]**  
**On-screen:** Subscribe + end screen  
Subscribe if you want the next video: how Vault points work, and how we run giveaways fair. Generators out. Real quests in. See you in the vault.
