## 0. Kill list — old Weebly / freesteamcodes21 patterns

Never revive these patterns on site, ads, YouTube, Discord, Facebook, or creator scripts:

| Banned pattern | Why |
|----------------|-----|
| “NO SURVEY OR DOWNLOAD” (or any claim that denies surveys/offers when they exist) | Trust nuke; partner/ad bans |
| “Working Steam codes 2026” / generators / hacks | Scam signal; illegal framing |
| Guaranteed free $X Steam / “instant free $50” | False promise; margin violation |
| Manual “do Code #1 → Contact Us → we email a code” | Unscalable, scammy, support hell |
| Asking for Steam passwords or account credentials | Fraud / account theft risk |
| gestyy / opaque shortlinks as primary CTAs | Spam reputation |
| Thin “PROOF WE ARE REAL” with no ledger, winners, or rules | Fake trust theater |
| Donation-as-product narrative | Not the earn path |
| Landing users on raw CPA when Vaultquest can host first | Disclosure + control loss |
| Fake urgency generators (“codes expire in 3 min”) | Deceptive UX |

**Migration rule:** Keep affiliate-funded rewards + YouTube/community. Replace fulfillment with in-house points, redeem catalog, and scheduled honest giveaways.

## 2. Proof & Rules page outline

**URL (suggested):** `/proof` or `/proof-and-rules`  
**IA role:** Trust surface — disclosures, how money works, rules, evidence. Not a card dump of fake badges.

### Page sections (in order)

1. **Headline + one sentence**  
   - Brand-forward. Subcopy: how Vaultquest funds rewards (partner commissions → points/giveaways).

2. **How earnings work**  
   - User completes partner quests → we receive commission (when offer qualifies) → points credit after verification → redeem or enter giveaways.  
   - Link to How it works for time expectations.

3. **What we never do**  
   - No generators, no Steam passwords, no contact-gated “Code #1” emails, no fake “working codes”.

4. **Giveaway rules**  
   - Eligibility, entry methods, schedule, winner selection method, announcement channel, prize delivery, void where prohibited.  
   - Link to live Giveaways page.

5. **Winner / redemption proof**  
   - Dated winners list; optional redacted redemption confirmations. Update on a stated cadence. Empty state: “First winners publish after [date]” — never invent proof.

6. **Partner & affiliate disclosure**  
   - We use third-party offer networks; some links are affiliate; we may earn when you complete offers.

7. **Account & anti-fraud rules (user-facing summary)**  
   - One account; no VPN/multi-accounting for fraud; no self-referrals; rewards may be on hold; violations → forfeit / ban. Full detail in Fraud policy + TOS.

8. **Creator / ad disclosure note**  
   - Videos and ads that promote Vaultquest should disclose sponsorship/affiliates; link to disclosure section below.

9. **Contact / Support**  
   - Support channel for account/redemption issues — **not** “email us your Freecash screenshot for a code”.

10. **Legal links**  
    - Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, (future) Cookie notice.

### Proof standards

- Prefer ledger-backed stats and published winners over screenshots alone.  
- Screenshots must be real, dated, and not implying Valve endorsement.  
- Never use stock “5-star trust” widgets or fake review counts.

## 3. Fraud policy

### Goals

Protect margin, partner relationships, and honest users. Detect clear abuse; **do not** falsely punish normal users (new device, travel, shared household edge cases) without review.

### What we detect / act on

| Signal | Typical action |
|--------|----------------|
| Multi-accounting / device farm patterns | Hold rewards → ban if confirmed |
| VPN / proxy / datacenter IP on high-risk offers (per partner rules) | Block credit or warn; educate on How it works |
| Self-referral / referral rings | Void referral bonuses; ban if repeated |
| Stolen payment / chargeback patterns on partners (via clawback) | Claw back points; restrict redemption |
| Chargebacks / rejected postbacks after credit | Reverse points; notify user when possible |
| Bot / automated offer completion | Ban |
| Giveaway multi-entry abuse | Disqualify entries; ban if severe |
| Credential stuffing / account takeover attempts | Lock account; require recovery |

### What we do **not** auto-punish without review

- Single VPN use on non-restricted content with otherwise clean history  
- Shared household IP if partners allow one account **and** user isn’t farming  
- Slow completers / low EPC users (not fraud)  
- First-time device change with matching email + normal behavior  

### User-facing consequences (escalation)

1. **Hold** — points/redemptions delayed pending verification  
2. **Void** — specific fraudulent credits removed  
3. **Restrict** — earn or redeem limited  
4. **Ban** — account closed; unpaid fraudulent balances forfeited  

### Ops rules

- Prefer **S2S postbacks** + clawback flags in ledger before paying Steam inventory.  
- Log fraud decisions (who/what/why) for support consistency.  
- Never coach users on bypassing partner rules.  
- Creator program: creators who teach fraud lose affiliate/creator status.

### Partner alignment

Mirror Freecash-class rules on UX copy: one account, no VPN/emulators/multi-accounting, no self-referrals, verification holds. Teaching rules ≠ coaching fraud.

## 4. Creator / ad disclosure requirements

### Always disclose when

- YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Discord, Facebook, or ads promote Vaultquest or partner offers  
- Any link can earn Vaultquest or the creator a commission  
- Content is paid, gifted, or incentivized by Vaultquest

### Required elements

| Placement | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| YouTube | Verbal disclosure early in video **and** written disclosure in description (first 1–3 lines). Example: “This video promotes Vaultquest; we may earn when you complete offers via our links.” |
| Short-form (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) | On-screen text + spoken line where practical; link sticker/bio disclosure |
| Paid ads (Meta/TikTok/Google) | Ad copy matches live offers; landing = Vaultquest; use platform “Paid partnership” / affiliate flags where required |
| Discord/Facebook | Affiliate/sponsor note in post or pinned channel rules |
| Site | Proof & Rules + footer affiliate disclosure |

### Creative rules

- Claims must match **live** Earn catalog (Marketing + Offers sync).  
- No “working codes” creatives.  
- Prefer traffic → **Vaultquest** first, then rotated partner links under the hood.  
- Creators must not promise personal code handouts or bypass the ledger.

### Creator agreement (minimum)

- Follow this claims policy  
- Use assigned tracked links / subIDs  
- No fraud coaching  
- Disclose relationship  
- Vaultquest may revoke links for policy breach

## 5. Age / geo notes

### Age

| Layer | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| Product ICP | ~13–35, but **comply with partner age gates** |
| Default site posture | **16+** minimum copy unless counsel/partners require **18+** |
| Offer walls / surveys | Enforce each network’s age requirement (often 18+ for money-adjacent offers) |
| Giveaways | State age eligibility explicitly; follow local prize/promotion rules |
| COPPA / kids | Do not target under-13; no child-directed design or ads |

**UX:** Age gate or checkbox where partners require it; never soft-lie about age to complete offers.

### Geo

- Offers are geo- and device-restricted; show availability honestly (“not available in your region”).  
- Do not instruct users to VPN into other geos to unlock offers.  
- Giveaways: “Void where prohibited”; exclude sanctioned / blocked jurisdictions as advised by counsel.  
- Paid ads: geo-target only regions where Earn path and fulfillment can actually deliver.  
- Steam rewards: respect Steam/gift-card regional constraints in redeem catalog copy.

### Data residency / transfer

Flag for Privacy Policy: analytics, email, hosting, and partner postbacks may process data in the US/EU — disclose in privacy outline; lawyer to finalize.

## 6. TOS / Privacy outline

**Not final legal text.** Shipable draft structure for Product/Engineering; **counsel recommended before scale** (see budget flag).

### Terms of Service — outline

1. Acceptance of terms; eligibility (age)  
2. Account registration; one-person / anti-abuse rules  
3. Description of service: quests, Vault points, redeem catalog, giveaways  
4. Points are **promotional credits**, not cash, not bank deposits; no cash-out except listed rewards  
5. Partner offers are third-party; Vaultquest not responsible for partner site content  
6. Reward holds, clawbacks, fraud voiding  
7. Giveaway official rules (or incorporate by reference)  
8. Prohibited conduct (fraud, harassment, IP theft, reverse engineering)  
9. Intellectual property; no Valve affiliation  
10. Disclaimers; limitation of liability  
11. Termination  
12. Governing law / disputes (**lawyer sets jurisdiction**)  
13. Contact  

### Privacy Policy — outline

1. Who we are / contact  
2. Data we collect: account, usage, click/postback IDs, device/IP, support messages  
3. Purposes: provide service, credit points, fraud prevention, analytics, marketing (with consent where required)  
4. Sharing: offer partners / affiliate networks, processors (email, hosting), legal requests  
5. Cookies / similar tech  
6. Retention  
7. Security (high-level, honest)  
8. Rights: access/delete/opt-out as applicable (CCPA/GDPR if you target those regions)  
9. Children’s privacy  
10. International transfers  
11. Changes to policy  

### Budget flag — lawyer needed?

| Item | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| Launch MVP TOS/Privacy | Agent-drafted outline → owner can use a reputable template generator **or** $100–300 lawyer/doc service for first pass |
| From `$1,000+` budget (`docs/08-budget.md`) | **Propose ~$150–400** for attorney review of TOS, Privacy, and giveaway rules **before** paid ads or large creator push |
| Cost / lift / kill | **Cost:** $150–400. **Lift:** reduces ban/takedown and chargeback risk; required for serious ad accounts. **Kill:** if staying organic-only micro-test &lt;$100 ads forever, delay counsel — but **do not** skip disclosure/claims policy either way |
| When lawyer is mandatory (practical) | Sweepstakes in restricted US states, GDPR-heavy EU marketing, handling real money beyond points, or formal creator contracts at scale |

**Decision for Wave 1:** Publish clear Proof & Rules + claims policy now; keep TOS/Privacy as “outline → template → counsel before paid scale.” Owner approves any legal spend under the expansion rule.
