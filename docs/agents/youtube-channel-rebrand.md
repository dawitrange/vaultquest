## Banner / profile art direction

### Specs

| Asset | Size (YouTube) | Safe zones |
|-------|----------------|------------|
| **Banner (channel art)** | 2560 × 1440 px master | Keep critical type/logo inside **1546 × 423** center safe area (desktop + TV + mobile crop). Avoid edges. |
| **Profile (avatar)** | 800 × 800 px (export); displays circular | Center mark; readable at 36–98 px |
| **Video watermark (optional)** | Transparent PNG, corner | Small wordmark or “VQ” monogram; teal on dark |

### Art direction — banner

- **Scene:** Full-bleed cold vault chamber or dim PC/gameplay atmosphere (graphite, steel, soft teal rim light). Same language as site hero — real texture, not purple AI sludge.
- **Brand:** Large **Vaultquest** wordmark (Syne Bold / matching site display) left or center of safe area — brand-first, not a tiny corner logo.
- **Line:** One short tagline only: *Earn gaming rewards honestly.* No stats, no fake badges (“LIVE CODES”), no floating stickers.
- **Accent:** Signal teal on wordmark underline or single latch/key glyph — brass reserved for a subtle vault latch detail, not warm cream backgrounds.
- **CTA strip (optional, bottom of safe area):** Site URL in mono or Sora Medium — no QR clutter.
- **Don’t:** Collage of Steam cards, neon casino chrome, “FREE $50 CODE” generator UI, emoji clusters, purple glow grids.

### Art direction — profile

- Circular crop of a **VQ** monogram or vault-latch mark on `--vq-bg-deep` with teal stroke.
- High contrast; legible at mobile size.
- No face photo required; if using creator face later, keep as secondary video branding only — channel identity stays Vaultquest.

### Color lock (match site)

- Background: `#0b1014` / `#11181f`
- Type: `#e8eef4`
- Accent: `#2dd4bf`
- Unlock metal (detail only): `#c4a574`
