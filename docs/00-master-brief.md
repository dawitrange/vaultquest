## Direction (locked)

**Hybrid Concept 3**
- **Product UX template:** Gamesbolt / Earnit (own accounts, points, quests/offers, redeem catalog, social proof)
- **Growth template:** Freecash-style creator funnels (YouTube/social → *our* site first, then partners)
- **Must-haves:** Automatic affiliate **link rotation**, **honest giveaways**, in-house points ledger

## Dual intent

| Stakeholder | Win condition |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Owner** | Maximize profit (partner commissions − redemption − fraud − support − ads) |
| **Customer** | Clear path to Steam credit / keys / giveaway entries via real tasks; honest time-vs-reward |

**Margin rule:** Never promise redemption that exceeds expected partner yield for that action. Giveaways are growth/trust COGS funded by surplus margin — not uncapped free codes.

## ICP

- PC gamers who want Steam wallet credit or cheap/free keys
- Ages ~13–35 (comply with partner age gates; default 16+/18+ by network)
- Discover via YouTube (“earn free Steam”), TikTok, Discord, Facebook, SEO
- Willing to do surveys / play mobile offers / watch videos if reward is credible

## Promise (public)

> Earn gaming rewards honestly. Complete quests, build Vault points, redeem Steam credit & keys — or enter fair giveaways. No generators. No password asks.

## Non-goals

- Magic Steam code generators or “guaranteed instant free $50”
- Claiming “no survey” when surveys/offers exist
- Manual “email us after Code #1” fulfillment
- Asking for Steam passwords
- Building a full casino / gambling product

## Claims policy (all agents)

**Allowed:** earn by completing partner tasks; typical time ranges; we earn when you complete offers; giveaway odds/rules.
**Banned:** “no survey/download” if false; “working codes 2026”; hacks; guaranteed amounts; fake urgency generators.
